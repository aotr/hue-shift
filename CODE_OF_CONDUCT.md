# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other space where you are representing this project.

## Expected

- Be specific and technical when you disagree. "This breaks X because Y" beats "this is wrong".
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the tool's scope.
- Give people room to be beginners. Everyone was one.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments about who someone is.
- Sexual attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at **animesh.aotr@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.

Responses scale with the behaviour: a private word, a request to edit or delete a comment, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who do not follow this policy face the same consequences as anyone else.
